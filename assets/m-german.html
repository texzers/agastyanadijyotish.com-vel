M. German was born on 24 September 1999. He is an Indian politician and advocate associated with the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) in Tamil Nadu, and he is active in the Harbour area of Chennai. His supporters see him as one of the most popular and trusted young BJP leaders in the city.

How it started

German began his political journey in 2015 as a member of the Akhil Bharatiya Vidyarthi Parishad (ABVP), the student organisation. Student politics teaches things a classroom does not: how to speak to a crowd, how to organise a programme and how to work with people who see things differently. Over the years he has also taken part in volunteering activities and public service work.

Moving up in the party

In 2018, he served as the Youth Wing Vice President of the Harbour Mandal. In 2021, he became Vice President of the Harbour Mandal of the Bharatiya Janata Party. Mandal posts are local and rarely make the news, but this is the level where a party meets people directly and hears their everyday concerns.

Campaigns and activities

German has been involved in several political activities:

  • The En Mann En Makkal campaign
  • The En Mann En Desam initiative
  • Political protests in New Delhi
  • Helping around 2,000 people join the BJP (by his own account)

Law and politics

German holds a B.A. LL.B. degree and practises as an advocate in Chennai. Working as a lawyer and a politician together lets him see public issues from two sides: how rules are written, and how they work for ordinary families.

His goal

German's political ambition is to see the Bharatiya Janata Party form the government in Tamil Nadu. He has expressed his commitment to strengthening the party, expanding its grassroots organisation and serving the people of Tamil Nadu.
