Understanding the Sacred Art

Agastya Nadi Jyotish is fundamentally rooted in the belief that your present life journey and past karmic actions are recorded on ancient Tamil palm leaves (Olai Chuvadi). Authored by Maharishi Agastya at Vaitheeswaran Koil, these leaves provide guidance when a seeker reaches a pivotal moment in life.
