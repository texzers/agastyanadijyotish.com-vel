What is Agastya Nadi Jyotish?

Agastya Nadi Jyotish is an ancient system of astrology preserved on dried palm leaves (Olai Chuvadi) written by Maharishi Agastya in Vaitheeswaran Koil, Tamil Nadu.

📜

Ancient Tamil Manuscripts

Written in poetic ancient Tamil, the manuscripts contain detailed insights into past karmas, present obstacles, and future destiny predictions.

👍

Thumb Impression Matching

Every human thumb impression is categorized into 108 micro-patterns. Your right (male) or left (female) thumb impression locates your bundle.
