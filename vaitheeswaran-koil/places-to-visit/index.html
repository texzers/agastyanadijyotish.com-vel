Beyond temples, the coastal region around Vaitheeswaran Koil offers rich heritage, beaches, and nature sanctuaries.

Poompuhar Beach & Heritage Museum

Distance: ~22 km
Ancient Chola port city mentioned in Silappatikaram epic. Features coastal views and art gallery museum.

Pichavaram Mangrove Forest

Distance: ~40 km
Second largest mangrove forest in the world. Ideal for serene boat rides through green canopy canals.
